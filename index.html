Fix the use case

A digital agentic solution to help patients and staff book outpatient appointments, follow up on referrals and answer insurance questions, in Arabic and English. Before anything is built, we decide how far the agent may go.

Agent autonomy level

For each task, select the autonomy level by pressing one of the three buttons. Decide as a room how far the agent may go, then compare with the reference.

The agent's brief

Role
Patient access coordinator for the outpatient clinics.
Goal
Every request ends in a booked or moved appointment, a tracked referral or a clear answer on coverage.
Context
Clinic directory, live booking system, referral inbox and insurance rules. Requests come in Arabic or English.
Rules & limits
The autonomy levels set above.
Output
A confirmed booking with a reference, or a short answer the patient understands.
Stop and ask
Red-flag symptoms go to the triage nurse. Anything that cannot be undone goes to the access coordinator in Teams.
